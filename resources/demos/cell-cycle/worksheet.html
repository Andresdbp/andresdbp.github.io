  2. Set the Speed slider to 2.0 h/s.

5Solve these without the demonstration

Close the page, or look away from it. Show your working. Then reopen it and check yourself, and where you were wrong, write down which step went off.

1. Use the undisturbed timings: G1 11 h, S 8 h, G2 4 h, M 1 h. A cell is 20.0 h into its cycle. Name the phase and say how far into that phase it is. This model cell has 6 chromosomes: at that moment, how many chromatids does each chromosome have, and how many DNA molecules are in the cell in total?

2. The restriction point falls about 7.5 h into G1. Growth factors are stripped from two identical cells: cell A at 5.0 h, cell B at 9.0 h. Predict what each does, and explain both outcomes in terms of cyclin D, p27 and Rb. Then state which of the two would be predicted incorrectly by the rule “no growth factor, no division.”

3. A division produces one daughter with 7 chromosomes and one with 5. Every gene present is intact and correctly sequenced, and both daughters have a complete copy of every chromosome they received. Name the checkpoint that failed and the exact point in mitosis at which it acts. Then explain why neither of the other two checkpoint failures could have produced this particular result.

4. At 100% growth factors the cycle is 24 h with an 11 h G1. At 45% growth factors G1 lasts about 27 h and nothing else changes. Calculate the total cycle length at 45%, and calculate the percentage of the cycle spent in G1 at each of the two signal levels. State what those two percentages imply about which part of the cycle a tissue actually regulates.

6On your own

Nothing here is graded, and the demonstration does considerably more than this worksheet uses.

  • Set Replication stress to 100% but leave the G2 checkpoint ticked. Time S phase and read card 2 while it holds. Then untick the checkpoint and run it again. Both cells have the same problem; only one of them survives it intact.
  • Set Spindle problems to 55% with the spindle assembly checkpoint left working. The cell parks at metaphase instead of dividing. That arrest is the mechanism by which taxol and the vinca alkaloids kill dividing cells — check the honest-limits panel for what a real cell does after several hours of it.
  • Tick Growth-factor independent with growth factors at 0% and run several divisions, watching Damage accumulated in this lineage. Then untick the G1 checkpoint, set DNA damage each cycle to 70%, and run several more. Note what the panel says about how many independent defects are present, and what it takes before the word cancer is warranted. The Several hits at once preset is the same idea taken further.
  • Watch the cyclin plot through one mitosis and find the moment cyclin B collapses. Name the event, name the enzyme complex responsible, and explain why the cell leaves mitosis because of a destruction rather than because of an elapsed time.
